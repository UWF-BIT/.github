## About Us

The **Business Innovation & Technology (BIT)** unit at [UW Facilities](https://facilities.uw.edu) collects, analyzes, and presents business data to foster innovation across the university's facilities operations. We build and maintain the tools, integrations, and data pipelines that support UW Facilities staff in making data-driven decisions.

BIT is organized into focused teams, each with ownership over specific applications and platforms:

| Team | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Business Intelligence** | ETL pipelines, data integration, and reporting & analytics |
| **Custom Development** | Internal web application development |
| **GIS** | Geographic information systems & spatial data |
| **Web** | Web presence & content management |
| **Information Technology** | Enterprise application support & administration |
| **Business Processes & Project Management** | Process improvement & organizational change |

## Key Services

- **AiM** — Enterprise asset & work order management system for UW Facilities operations
- **GIS Maps** — Public and secure mapping of UW properties, buildings, and spaces
- **Space Manager** — Building space allocation management tools
- **Hybrid Workspace Scheduling** — Desk and workspace reservation tools for UW staff
- **Data Integration & Analytics** — ETL pipelines, reporting dashboards, and data governance
